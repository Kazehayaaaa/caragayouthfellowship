CYF Event Rules & Regulations

By participating in this event, you agree to follow the rules and guidelines established by the CYF organizers.

  • Be Present and On Time: Attend all scheduled activities, sessions, and meals promptly.
  • Limit Distractions: Keep gadget use to a minimum and maintain silence during spiritual sessions or quiet times.
  • Respect Everyone: Treat all staff, leaders, and fellow participants with respect, sportsmanship, and kindness.
  • Behavior and Accountability: Always maintain proper conduct; misbehavior will be dealt with firmly, and you are personally responsible for any damage to people, property, or the host venue.
  • Respect Sleeping Areas: Stay out of quarters designated for the opposite gender.
  • Wear Your ID: Keep your event name tag or ID visible at all times.
  • Stay On-Site: Do not leave the venue without express permission from staff.
  • No Substances: Smoking, drinking alcohol, and taking illegal drugs are strictly forbidden.
  • Dress Appropriately: Wear suitable clothing for each occasion; keep casual or athletic wear for sports and free time.
  • Take Care of Property: Keep your items safe, do not touch unauthorized equipment or instruments, keep the grounds clean, and follow all venue and staff rules.

🔒 Data Privacy Notice

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), its Implementing Rules and Regulations, and the issuances of the National Privacy Commission (NPC), CYF collects and processes your personal information only for:

  • event registration and participant management;
  • communication, payment processing, and event administration;
  • safety, emergency response, and related CYF purposes.

Access is limited to authorized personnel and service providers necessary to run this system. As a data subject you have the right to be informed, to access, correct, object to, and request erasure or blocking of your data, and to file a complaint with the NPC (privacy.gov.ph). If you are a minor, a parent or guardian should review this notice with you.
