About CYF

A fellowship for the young people of Caraga.

Caraga Youth Fellowship (CYF) is the youth ministry of the Caraga Fellowship of Fundamental Baptist Churches (CFFBC). We bring young people from local churches across the region together through fellowship, conferences, youth camps and ministry.

Everything we do follows three words: transform, equip and engage.

Transform

Hearts changed by the Gospel, as young people grow in their faith and walk with God.

Equip

Training young leaders through the Word, so they can serve their churches with conviction and skill.

Engage

Reaching out together, serving local churches and communities in every province of Caraga.
