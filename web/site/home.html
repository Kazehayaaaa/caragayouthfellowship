About CYF · Who We Are

Caraga Youth Fellowship

Caraga Youth Fellowship (CYF) is the youth ministry arm of the Caraga Fundamental Baptist Fellowship of Churches (CFFBC), serving young people across its fellowship of churches.

CYF brings together young people from CFFBC churches for fellowship, discipleship, training, service, and shared ministry experiences. We seek to strengthen what God is already doing through the local church by creating opportunities for young people to grow together, serve together, and take part in ministry together.

CYF shares the doctrinal identity of CFFBC and its member churches. We therefore understand our ministry within the biblical and ministry convictions of the churches we serve.

CYF is not a separate church.

We exist to serve young people within the fellowship and to work alongside local churches as they disciple and care for the next generation.

Our Mission

We exist to confront the modern challenges of youth through sound preaching of the Gospel, discipleship, character development, and holistic personal growth.

CYF seeks to meet young people where they are while remaining anchored in biblical truth. Through Gospel-centered ministry, intentional discipleship, character formation, and practical care for the whole person, we help young people respond to the realities they face with faith, wisdom, and Christlike character.

Our Vision

We envision cultivating spiritual maturity and Christlikeness in youth, equipping them to become effective servant-leaders and disciple-makers.

Our vision is for young people to grow beyond participation into faithful, capable service. As they mature in Christ, we want them to develop the character, understanding, and practical ability to serve others, lead with humility, and help make disciples.

How We Develop

Transform · Equip · Entrust

CYF understands the development of young people as a work that ultimately belongs to God. He is the One who transforms, equips, and entrusts; CYF serves as a ministry instrument in His work.

1

TRANSFORM

God changes lives.

CYF points young people to Christ and His Word, trusting God to transform their lives, character, convictions, and relationships.

2

EQUIP

Growth requires preparation.

CYF helps young people develop the biblical understanding, character, practical skills, and confidence they need to serve faithfully.

3

ENTRUST

What is developed is given responsibility.

CYF creates opportunities for young people to serve, lead, contribute, and make disciples as they grow in maturity and capability.

Transform · Equip · Entrust is not a formula for producing mature Christians. It is a framework for how CYF participates in God's work in and through young people.

What Shapes Us · S.E.R.V.A.N.T.

Called to follow, equipped to lead, born to be Christ’s SERVANT.

Our core values shape how we grow, lead, serve, and disciple.

What We Do

Six enduring areas of ministry.

These areas remain constant even as particular ministries, programs, and methods change to meet the needs of each generation.

1. CHRIST-CENTERED FORMATION

Who are they becoming in Christ?

CYF cultivates spiritual maturity and Christlikeness by helping young people develop lives increasingly shaped by Christ, Scripture, biblical convictions, faithful Christian living, and a biblical understanding of the world.

2. WHOLE-PERSON CARE

How are we taking care of them?

CYF intentionally cares for young people as whole persons by cultivating safe, trustworthy, supportive environments and providing appropriate guidance, counseling, protection, referral, and follow-up.

3. CAPABILITY DEVELOPMENT

What can they become capable of doing?

CYF develops usable competence rather than merely encouraging willingness. Young people are trained, given opportunities to practice, corrected, coached, and equipped to demonstrate capability in real ministry and life situations.

4. RESPONSIBILITY & SERVANT LEADERSHIP

What can they faithfully carry?

CYF progressively moves young people from participation toward meaningful responsibility and servant leadership according to their maturity, capability, readiness, and faithfulness.

5. LOCAL-CHURCH STRENGTHENING

How does CYF strengthen the churches?

CYF serves as the youth ministry arm of CFFBC and seeks to strengthen its member churches in caring for, developing, equipping, and preparing their young people.

6. GENERATIONAL MULTIPLICATION

What happens after us?

CYF intentionally develops people, leaders, capabilities, systems, and ministry capacity that can be transferred and reproduced across generations.

INTERCHURCH CONNECTION & COLLABORATION

Interchurch connection remains an important and distinctive expression of CYF's identity as a fellowship of CFFBC churches. CYF creates opportunities for churches and young people to fellowship across local-church boundaries, learn from one another, share experience and resources, collaborate in ministry, develop relationships, participate in shared training and events, and experience a broader CFFBC youth community.

Connection is an expression; strengthening the churches is the enduring function.

Where We Belong

Serving the church, strengthening the next generation.

CYF is the youth ministry arm of the Caraga Fundamental Baptist Fellowship of Churches (CFFBC). We serve young people across the fellowship while working alongside local churches to help them care for, develop, equip, and prepare their young people for faithful service.

CYF provides a shared space where churches can learn from one another, share resources and experience, develop ministry capacity, and give young people opportunities to grow and serve together. Through shared training, fellowship, ministry experiences, and collaboration, we seek to strengthen what God is already doing through the local church.

CYF does not replace the local church. We seek to strengthen and support the churches we belong to, helping them become more capable of caring for and developing the young people entrusted to them.

We believe that CYF is strongest when it strengthens the churches it serves.
